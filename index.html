Design rationale

Ending well is a design problem.

A CSP leaving Wiom is a person losing a livelihood relationship. The design job is to make the ending legible, dignified, and honest — the CSP always knows where they stand, why, and what happens to their money and devices. Three principles do the heavy lifting.

Blocker

Wiom-initiated = full-page

The CSP never opted in, so B1/B2 open as a full-page Notice (सूचना) — a red kicker + rule, one bold statement, one action. It cannot be dismissed into the feed.

Opt-in

Voluntary = consequences first

A self-exit is a legitimate choice. It lives quietly in Settings, opens a double opt-in (read consequences → explicit second confirm), and stays cancellable.

One line

Reason in one honest line

The कारण-… line is orange; the decision headline and dates are black. No walls of text — the CSP reads the why in a glance.

Disclosure ceiling — Exit OS §07

The app shows state + timeline, never the internal risk classification. B1 says the work stayed below target and links to सेवा स्थिति so the CSP can see the metric themselves — it never exposes scores, rankings, or the model that flagged them.

FPV / Enforcement OS — the B2 exception

For integrity exits the Enforcement OS · FPV note (13 Jul 2026) overrides the ceiling: the CSP is shown the actual violation type so the decision isn't arbitrary and can be contested. Types: Disintermediation, Extra Cash (out-of-system payment — ग्राहक से तय कीमत से ज़्यादा पैसे लिए गए), Device Lost / Theft / Damage. A human still walks them through it — the note stays.

Implementation

One endpoint, one model, seven states.

The app never decides an exit — it renders GET /exit/status. The banner arrives on a separate /feed/banners channel and deep-links in. Everything below is server-rendered; the client composes nothing.

Endpoints

CallEffect
GET /exit/statusKeyed on exitState → the fixture above.
POST /exit/declareSets exitState=declared + taskFeedComposition=exit_active → home banner appears.
POST /exit/abortClears both keys → banner disappears, back to normal home.
GET /feed/bannersServes banners__exit_active.json; overrides the feed banner. This is what puts the exit banner on Home.

Product intent

Why this exists.

WHY
A partnership ending — by either side — must be clear and dignified, not a silent shutdown. The CSP always knows where they stand, why, and what happens to their money and devices.
WHO
Channel Service Partners (CSPs). Voluntary = the CSP choosing to leave. B1/B2 = Wiom ending the relationship.
WHAT (success)
The CSP reaches settlement + device return with zero surprise: no un-explained loss of work, no disputed money, no "why was I removed?" support ticket.

What happens to pending work at exit

The most common CSP fear: "I have live installs, services and recharges — what happens to them?" The answer is the same across all three classes — no work is orphaned.

Work typeOn exitWho finishes it
New / unassignedRouting stops — none is created for the exiting CSP.—
In-flight installNear-complete jobs may finish within the notice window; the rest are taken over.Wiom का सिस्टम
Open service / restoreHanded to Wiom's system; the customer never sees a gap.Wiom टीम
Pending rechargeSettled; commission reconciled into the final settlement.Settlement (S5)
CustomersMigrated to another partner during S4 — irreversible once started.Wiom

Timeline windows

Voluntary · ~6 weeks

16 जुलाई declare → 16–30 जुलाई नोटिस → 30 जुलाई–2 अगस्त काम बंद → 2–23 अगस्त ग्राहक माइग्रेशन → 23–30 अगस्त सेटलमेंट → 30 अगस्त पूरा

Longer, gentler — the CSP chose this, and can still turn back until migration.

B1 / B2 · ~2 weeks

16 जुलाई शुरू → काम तुरंत बंद → 23–30 जुलाई सेटलमेंट → 30 जुलाई पूरा

Faster — Wiom initiated, work stops immediately, no notice period.
